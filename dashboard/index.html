How the implied value is built

  • Size discount — a launch floats a marginal token supply, so a bigger pool dilutes: implied value per dollar of TVL falls with TVL.
  • Momentum — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60…+120%, lifts or trims the multiple.
  • Age — protocols that ran longest without a token have less launch left; young ones get more optionality.
  • Completeness — rows whose TVL, growth and listing date are all reported score higher: the signal is verifiable.

What a farm step costs

Every plan names the capital, the gas on the chain named (Ethereum $10–25, elsewhere under $1), the weekly cadence and a hold window sized to the protocol's age — 90 days for the youngest, 45 for the rest. Nothing in a plan pays a third party.
